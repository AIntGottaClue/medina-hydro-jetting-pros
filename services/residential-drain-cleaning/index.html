What this covers

Home lines, cleaned to the wall.

Household drains collect grease, soap, lint, scale, and roots a little at a time. By the time a line backs up, the pipe has often been narrowing for years. Snaking opens a channel. Jetting removes the buildup that made the channel necessary.

We start with a camera so you know what is in the pipe, set the nozzle and pressure for the pipe material, and finish with a second camera pass.

  • Kitchen and laundry line jetting
  • Main line root and scale removal
  • Whole-home slow-drain diagnostics

Signs it is time

When a home line needs jetting.

Repeat clogs

The same drain clears and slows again within weeks or months. That points to buildup on the wall, not a one-time blockage.

Gurgling and slow fixtures

Noise from drains and several fixtures slowing at once often means the main line is narrowed.

Backups at the lowest drain

A basement floor drain or lowest tub backing up is a classic sign of a restricted main lateral.

Not sure if it is a drain or the main? Describe it on the form and we will help you sort it out. Related: sewer camera inspection.

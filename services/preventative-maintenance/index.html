Part 01

Begin with a baseline

One camera pass records the material, joints and buildup. Later visits compare against it. Keep the footage with your property records and note where the cleanout is.

It also gives you something to show if a repair question comes up.

Part 02

Set the interval by use

A restaurant kitchen line needs a shorter interval than a spare bathroom. A line with a history of roots needs more checking than one with a clean record.

There is no single schedule for every property, so we base it on the footage and your backup history.

Part 03

What maintenance cannot do

Cleaning keeps a sound line sound. It does not repair a break, replace a section or service a septic system. If the footage shows damage, we say so and you decide what comes next.

Questions

Preventative Maintenance: common questions.

How often should I schedule jetting?

It depends on use, pipe condition and past backups. A baseline camera pass helps set it.

Is maintenance needed if there are no backups?

Properties with grease, roots or repeat clogs benefit most.

Does maintenance replace repair?

No. It keeps a sound line clear and shows when repair is needed.

Service 01

Hydro Jetting

The core of everything we do. A high-pressure hose and nozzle assembly runs the full length of the line, scouring the pipe wall clean instead of punching a temporary hole through the clog.

  • Main sewer laterals and interior drain lines
  • Recurring maintenance jetting for problem lines
  • Post-jet camera verification on every line

Service 02

Residential Drain Cleaning

A slow kitchen sink, a gurgling laundry line, a main that backs up every few months. Residential lines collect grease, soap, scale, and roots year after year. Jetting resets the line to full diameter.

  • Kitchen and laundry line jetting
  • Main line root and scale removal
  • Whole-home slow-drain diagnostics

Service 03

Commercial and Grease Lines

Restaurants and commercial kitchens push grease through their lines every day. Left alone, it hardens, narrows the line, and eventually closes it, usually during a rush.

  • Grease-laden kitchen lines
  • Floor drains and mop sink lines
  • Recurring maintenance programs

More problems we clear

Specific causes, specific fixes.

Tree Root Intrusions

Roots work into a lateral at the joints, then thicken until the line stops. We cut them out of the pipe with a jet and film what is left, so you know whether the line needs repair.

Read more

Severe Grease and Sludge

Grease sets on the pipe wall and keeps collecting soap, food and sediment. A jet strips it back to the wall, and the camera confirms what came out.

Read more

Mineral and Scale Deposits

Scale is a hard, rough layer that forms inside older pipe. It narrows the line a little at a time and catches whatever else passes through.

Read more

Recurring Clogs and Slow Drains

If the same drain clogs again in a few weeks, the first fix did not touch the cause. We look at the pattern, film the line and clean the wall.

Hydro Jetting for Medfair Heights Apartment Buildings

The National Park Service lists the Medfair Heights Apartment Historic District at 221 North State Street, with 1943 as its significant year, the National Housing Agency among its architects and significance in architecture, community planning and social history. It was listed in 2008, one of five historic districts and places the register lists for Medina County.

Multi-building apartment complexes share drain infrastructure. A kitchen stack serves several units, and building sewers can carry the output of many households. A blockage low in a stack may show up in the lowest unit first, though its source may be several floors up.

Jetting can clear a sound shared line of grease and scale, and an inspection should come first, especially in buildings of this age. The city's brochure covers the review process for locally designated properties, which does not apply automatically to every historic listing.

Where Hydro Jetting Comes Up in Medfair Heights

Shared kitchen stacks

One stack can serve several kitchens. A film building up on it slows every unit it connects.

Building sewers

The line out of a building carries the load of all its units. Roots or scale there affect everyone.

Older buildings with long service lives

Buildings from the 1940s have been maintained for decades. The pipe may have been patched at many points.

Common-area drains

Laundry rooms and basement drains share the building line.

Hydro jetting questions in Medfair Heights

Who approves jetting in an apartment building?

The owner or property manager.

Can one stoppage affect many units?

Yes, if the blockage is on a shared line.

Is hydro jetting safe on older pipe?

On sound pipe it can be. A damaged section should be assessed first.

Will cleaning repair a broken pipe?

No. Repair is separate.

Why does my basement drain back up first?

It sits at the low point of the system.

Could the problem be the public sewer?

If neighbors see the same backup, it may be. If only your property is affected, start with the private line.

What does a camera inspection show?

Pipe material, joints, roots, deposits and damage.

How often should I clean?

It depends on the cause.

Will one cleaning stop the problem for good?

It clears the buildup present at that visit. Whether it returns depends on the cause.

What should I have ready?

Your address, the affected fixtures, when it began and any repair notes.

Neighborhoods / West Liberty Street

Hydro Jetting Along West Liberty Street

The Beyond the Storefronts project notes that West Liberty was one of the first streets paved out from the square. The city's comprehensive plan names the West Liberty Corridor as a focus area and says small multi-use zones along West Liberty, North Court, North Broadway and Washington Street allow older homes to be repurposed as businesses.

The Library of Congress record for Medina Public Square places the square's boundary along Liberty Street, so this corridor runs straight out from the historic center. A house turned office can keep residential pipe while carrying a different load. The same plan says industrial zones sit west of downtown, so some properties here may sit near heavier uses and some near homes.

Whatever the use, hydro jetting suits sound lines with buildup, and an inspection decides.

Hydro jetting questions in West Liberty Street

Can an old house used as an office have more drain trouble?

It can, since more people use pipe that was sized for a household.

Does a business need to tell the crew what goes down the drain?

Yes. It affects how the line is cleaned.

Is hydro jetting safe on older pipe?

On sound pipe it can be. A damaged section should be assessed first.

Will cleaning repair a broken pipe?

No. Repair is separate.

Why does my basement drain back up first?

It sits at the low point of the system.

Could the problem be the public sewer?

If neighbors see the same backup, it may be. If only your property is affected, start with the private line.

What does a camera inspection show?

Pipe material, joints, roots, deposits and damage.

How often should I clean?

It depends on the cause.

Will one cleaning stop the problem for good?

It clears the buildup present at that visit. Whether it returns depends on the cause.

What should I have ready?

Your address, the affected fixtures, when it began and any repair notes.

01

Inspect

Every job starts with eyes inside the line. A sewer camera shows what is actually causing the problem (roots, grease, scale, a sag, or a break) so nothing downstream is guesswork.

02

Calibrate

Pressure and nozzle selection are matched to the pipe: its material, diameter, and condition. A fragile line and a clay main full of roots are two very different jobs, and the equipment is set accordingly.

03

Scour

The jetter runs the line end to end. Rear-facing jets pull the hose forward while the stream strips buildup off the walls and flushes the debris out of the line.

04

Verify

The camera runs again after the jet. You see the cleaned line yourself. That footage is the proof the job is done, not a promise that it is.

Why the order matters

The camera is the contract.

Jetting without a camera is pressure without information. Inspecting first keeps fragile pipe safe, and verifying after turns "we cleared it" into "watch the footage."

That is why the same four stages run on every line in Medina, whether it is a residential lateral or a commercial grease run.
